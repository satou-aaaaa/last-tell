Privacy Policy

Effective: October 9, 2026　Last updated: October 9, 2026

This page explains how the game "LAST TELL" (the "Game") handles information about its players. The Game is available as a web version (played in a browser or added to the home screen) and as an app distributed on the App Store and Google Play.

1. Who we are

satou (individual developer)
Contact: the email address listed on the Support page

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location. The Game has no accounts or logins, and we do not run any servers.

Your save data (progress, settings, notes and nickname) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it with "More → Delete save data" in the Game, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing your browser's site data.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to investigate problems.

4. Ads and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent at launch and uses ads and analytics only within what you agree to. You can change your choice at any time in "More → Privacy settings".
  • On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks whether you allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising ID is not used. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (the location may vary by device).
  • The Game does not show gambling ads.

5. For users in the EEA and the UK (GDPR)

  • The only personal data we hold ourselves is email you send us.
  • Processing for ads and analytics is based on your consent. Crash reporting is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working.
  • Data may be processed outside Europe, including in the United States, by Google LLC and others, under mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, data portability and to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. For data handled by Google's services, you may also contact those services directly.

6. Age

The Game contains simulated gambling (poker) and is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. No real money

In-game chips are points only. They cannot be exchanged for money or prizes and are not sold.

8. Changes

When we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will also give notice in the Game.
