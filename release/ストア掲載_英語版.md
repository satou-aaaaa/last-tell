## Full description（4000文字以内）
```
In a bar at the edge of the harbor, a rookie who can't stop losing meets an old man.
"...You don't even know how to lose properly."
His name is Jin. Fifteen years ago, he was a legend at the table — until he vanished.

LAST TELL is a single-player Texas Hold'em story game. Become Jin's apprentice and take on the city's poker clubs one at a time.

■ A MENTOR AT YOUR SIDE
Never played poker? No problem. In Chapter 1, Jin walks you through hand rankings and when to fold, one move at a time. Each chapter teaches a new way to win: stealing the blinds, bluffing, catching a lie.

■ READ THE TELL
A glance at the chips when the hand is strong. Sudden chatter when it's weak.
Every opponent has a habit that gives away their hand. Watch their faces and gestures, and see through the bluff.

■ NEW CLUBS, NEW RULES
A harbor bar, an old-school club, a jazz club, a hotel lounge. Every venue has its boss and its house rules — straddles, bomb pots and more.

■ WHAT HAPPENED FIFTEEN YEARS AGO?
At every club, Jin's old friends hint at "that game." Why did he leave the table? And what does the title, LAST TELL, really mean?

■ FAIR, AND NO PAY-TO-WIN
In story chapters, the deal is adjusted to keep the story moving. In free play, cards are shuffled fairly every hand. Nothing you can buy makes you stronger. Chips cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

■ FOR PLAYERS WHO
- want to learn poker
- love mind games and reading people
- want a card game with a real story
- want to play offline, at their own pace

This game contains simulated gambling. No real money or prizes can be won.
```
